📜 Game Rules

🎮 How to Play

  • 🗺️ Journey through 10 regions — each with 5 gyms
  • ❓ Each gym has 10 questions from 10 different categories
  • ⏱️ Answer before the timer runs out!
  • 💎 Meet the minimum crystal threshold to earn a Badge

🔮 Crystal System

  • ✅ Correct answer = Base crystals
  • ⚡ Faster answer = Speed bonus crystals
  • 🏆 Win a gym = Round winner bonus
  • 👑 Complete all regions = Grand Champion bonus

🃏 Difficulty Tiers

⬜ Basic10s · Easy
✨ Holo12s · Moderate
💎 Rare15s · Challenging
🌟 Super Rare18s · Hard
👑 Ultra Rare20s · Legendary

🐾 Pokemon System

  • 🎁 Start with 3 free Pokeballs
  • 🔴 Buy more Pokeballs with crystals
  • ⭐ Unused Pokemon gain levels each region
  • 💎 Level 5 Pokemon = massive crystal reward at end!

💰 Prize

  • Every 100 🔮 = ₱1 in Pokemon card credits
  • Spend at the convention on any cards you want!
